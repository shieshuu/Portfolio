🌐 Internship Project · CloudSwyft

Pre-Assessment Website

A web-based assessment developed during my internship at CloudSwyft, in collaboration with my co-intern.

About the project

A web-based FRS Pre-Assessment developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript during my internship at CloudSwyft, in collaboration with my co-intern. The assessment helps users discover an IT Future Ready Skills (FRS) track that aligns with their interests, strengths, skills, and preferences.

Users answer situational questions using a rating scale, and their responses are used to provide personalized FRS track recommendations. The assessment serves as a starting point for career and learning exploration rather than a definitive evaluation of a person's abilities or career suitability.

About the project

An interactive digital contract experience designed for clients to easily provide their details and complete an agreement online. The system walks the client through four clear steps — entering personal information, selecting preferred dates, signing on a digital sign board, and confirming security.

Each step is designed with usability in mind, making the contract process feel less formal and more engaging while keeping the information accurate, organized, and secure.

About the e-book

An interactive flipbook developed as part of our internship requirements, where each intern was assigned a specific value to create an educational e-book around. The e-book features animated illustrations and interactive page transitions to make the story more engaging for young readers.

It also includes English and Tagalog narration options, allowing readers to choose their preferred language while following the story and learning about the assigned value.
